Nine Questions Before Building

A

Money and billing

These decide what a fee is, and therefore what realization actually divides.

A1

What currency are engagement fees in — and do historical contracts mix the two?

Changes — whether fee_currency can be defaulted or has to be carried and converted on every row of the realization view.

A2

Are engagements fixed-fee only, or are some time-and-materials?

Changes — for a fixed fee, realization is fee ÷ hours. For T&M the fee is hours × rate, so realization is meaningless and the view must show budget variance instead. If both exist, the stream needs a billing model.

A3

Can an internal stream ever be billable — development done for a client's benefit that is chargeable?

Changes — whether kind='internal' can hard-force billable=false, or whether billability has to stay a per-entry decision that the picker must surface.

B

Scope of tracking

These decide which rows exist at all, and how granular they are.

B1

Contractor hours too, or only your own?

Changes — whether time_entry needs a person dimension and cost rates. Realization computed on your hours alone is wrong the moment someone else bills to the engagement.

B2

Does an entry ever need to attach to a specific Notion task, or is stream-level enough for now?

Changes — whether the Notion write-back can roll up to the engagement, or has to resolve individual task rows — and whether the picker needs a second level under each engagement.

B3

Which internal and admin streams do you actually want on day one?

Changes — the seed data, and how coarse your utilization split is. Too few and everything lands in one bucket; too many and you hesitate at the picker. Edit the list below.

C

Data quality rules

These decide how the app behaves when the record is imperfect — which, by your own account, is most of the time.

C1

What are the working-day boundaries for gap detection, and do weekends differ?

Changes — what counts as unaccounted time in the Day view. Set them too wide and every evening reads as a gap; too narrow and real early or late work is invisible.

C2

Is reconstructed time acceptable as evidence in your own client files, or must the export be able to exclude it?

Changes — whether the timesheet export needs a filter and a second set of totals. This is the one question with a professional-standards answer rather than a preference — worth being conservative.

C3

How far back should backfill be allowed without a hard block?

Changes — the confirmation threshold on backdated entries. A hard block prevents accidents; it also prevents entering the week you spent at a client site with no phone.
